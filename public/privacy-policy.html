Privacy Policy

Developer: Wakjira

Apps Covered: "Are You Smarter Than a 7-Year-Old?" (com.wakjira.smarter7yo) and "Talk to the Greatest Minds" (com.wakjira.greatestminds)

We built our applications on an Offline-First, Zero-Login architecture. You never need to create an account, provide an email address, or share personal identity data to use our apps.

1. Zero Personal Data Collection & Local Storage

  • All game runs, scholarship report cards, unlocked badges, daily streaks, and mentor chat transcripts are stored strictly on your device using local sandboxed storage.
  • You can export your local records anytime to CSV, JSON, or printable HTML, or wipe all local data immediately via the in-app "Clear All History" button or by uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising & EU/GDPR Consent (Google UMP)

  • Free versions display ads via Google AdMob. Before initializing the MobileAds SDK, our consent dialog lets you choose between personalized or contextual (non-personalized) ads.
  • Purchasing the one-time Pro Unlock ($3.99) permanently removes all banner and interstitial ads.

3. Generative AI Content Disclaimer & Reporting

  • Historical and parody personas in "Talk to the Greatest Minds" provide educational and entertainment perspectives only and do not constitute medical, legal, or financial advice.
  • Users can flag or report any generated message directly inside the chat view.
