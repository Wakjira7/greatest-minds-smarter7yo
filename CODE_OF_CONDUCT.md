# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our project and community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:
- Demonstrating empathy, kindness, and constructive engineering feedback
- Respecting cultural and linguistic diversity across all supported global languages
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive code review feedback

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:
- Harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks
- Publishing private information or credentials without permission
- Submitting unauthorized copyrighted material or malicious code

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer at **`wakjira7@gmail.com`**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
